About AFCA Movements

AFCA Movements is an Afro-Caribbean based dance group that celebrates the rich heritage, rhythms, and traditions of Afro-Caribbean cultures. We are passionate about preserving and sharing the vibrant dance traditions that connect us to our roots.

🌍

African Roots

Honoring the deep cultural heritage and traditional movements passed down through generations.

🎭

Cultural Expression

Celebrating the diversity and beauty of Afro-Caribbean dance forms and musical traditions.

🤝

Community Spirit

Building connections and fostering unity through the power of dance and shared cultural experiences.

Our Performances

AFCA Movements delivers powerful and dynamic performances that showcase the richness of Afro-Caribbean dance. Our shows combine traditional movements with contemporary expression, creating unforgettable experiences for audiences.

Workshops & Classes

Join us for immersive workshops where you can learn traditional Afro-Caribbean dance techniques, explore the cultural significance of movements, and connect with a vibrant community of dancers.

Dancer Recruitment Classes (18+)

Calling all dancers aged 18 and over! Join our recruitment classes designed to find talented performers for our upcoming shows and performances.

These sessions are perfect for experienced dancers looking to join AFCA Movements and perform at festivals, cultural events, and community celebrations. Show us your skills and passion for Afro-Caribbean dance!

Performance Recruitment Sessions (18+)

Are you an experienced dancer ready to take the stage? Our performance recruitment sessions are open to dancers aged 18 and over who want to join our performance troupe.

These classes focus on auditioning and selecting dancers for our upcoming performances. Bring your energy, technique, and passion for Afro-Caribbean dance culture—we're looking for dedicated performers to join our team!
