About Dingolay 2026

Dingolay 2026 is a vibrant celebration of Caribbean dance culture, drumming traditions, and the rich heritage of the Windrush generation. This community-focused workshop brings together people of all ages to learn, celebrate, and preserve the beautiful traditions of Caribbean carnival culture.

Through dance, rhythm, and storytelling, we honor the contributions of the Windrush generation and explore the dynamic world of Caribbean carnival arts. Join us for an immersive experience that connects communities through culture, movement, and music.
